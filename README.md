# Aaj

**Three things, today.**

Write down what's on your mind, or say it. Aaj shows you three tasks at a time, and every task gets resolved within a day.

Live at **[aaj.namastevis.in](https://aaj.namastevis.in)**

## How it works
1. **Empty your head.** Type or speak each task. No time or priority yet.
2. **Size it up.** For each task: does it matter today, and roughly how long will it take, in 25-minute blocks?
   - Matters today, one block: a **quick win**.
   - Matters today, two or more blocks: a **big task**.
   - Doesn't matter today, one block: a **fill-in**, shown only once the important work is done.
   - Doesn't matter today, two or more blocks: a **time sink**. Aaj asks you to hand it off or drop it before your day starts.

   You can't plan more work than the hours left today.
3. **Your three.** One big task, marked "Start here", and two quick wins for your breaks. The rest wait, out of sight. Resolve one and the next slides in.

Every task leaves in one of four ways: **done**, **handed off**, **scheduled** or **dropped**. These are the four actions of the Eisenhower matrix. After 24 hours a task turns red, and you can't add anything new until it's resolved.

The timer works in Pomodoro blocks: 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of break. When a task's blocks run out, you choose: done, one more block, hand off, schedule or drop. Swipe a task right to mark it done.

## Principles
- No sign-up, no accounts, no tracking, no ads.
- Nothing is kept. Open tasks live only on your device, and each one is deleted the moment it's resolved. There is no history, archive or export.
- Voice input uses your phone's own speech service (Chrome on Android, Safari on iPhone), which may send audio to Google or Apple to turn it into text. Where it isn't available, the mic button doesn't appear.
- Works offline, and can be added to the home screen.

## Files
One HTML file and three small helpers: `index.html` (the whole app), `manifest.webmanifest`, `icon.svg`, and `sw.js` (offline support). No build step, no server. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
